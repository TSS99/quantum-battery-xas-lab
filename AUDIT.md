## Findings

1. **Wrong; applied.** `src/components/beamline-scene.tsx:43,173`, `src/components/beamline-panel.tsx:29,41-42`. The sample legend says it “absorbs” the fraction `1 - T`, but the input coefficient is `mu(...).total`, including coherent and incoherent scattering. This is the fraction removed from the direct beam, not the photoabsorbed fraction. **Exact fix:** say “removes … from the direct beam” and explain next to the default plot that the displayed coefficient is total attenuation. Keep the browser-tested “Absorption” controls and metric text. No change to counts. See [NIST attenuation law](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/XrayMassCoef/chap2.html) and [XrayDB cross-section kinds](https://xraypy.github.io/XrayDB/python.html#xraydb.mu_elam).

2. **Misleading; applied.** `src/components/beamline-scene.tsx:28-30,171,178`, `README.md:20`. The numeric Bragg angle is correct, but the drawing clamps it to 7–40 degrees. The claim that the drawn crystal angle follows the live value is incomplete outside that interval. With the specified spacing, first-order reflection requires `E >= hc/(2d) = 1977.041... eV`; the 2100 eV switch is a schematic instrument choice, not that physical cutoff. **Exact fix:** retain the valid numeric angle and geometry, explicitly label the drawn angle as constrained for legibility, and distinguish the Bragg readout from the schematic drawing in README. [ALBA's Si(111) spacing](https://www.cells.es/en/instruments/beamlines/bl11-ncd-sweet/layout-1) agrees with 3.1356 Å. No instrument-performance claim is inferred from Bragg's law alone.

3. **Misleading; applied.** `src/components/beamline-scene.tsx:36,140,155-158,174,178`. Fluorescence is an on/off illustration controlled only by `energy >= edgeEnergy`. It uses no shell yield, absorption probability, detector efficiency or self-absorption, although the text states that the sample emits X-rays above every selected edge. **Exact fix:** preserve “Above the … edge” and “Below the … edge” text, describe the above-edge fluorescence as schematic, and state that fluorescence intensity is not calculated. This also covers shells with zero tabulated yield. No invented fluorescence model is added.

4. **Wrong; applied.** `src/lib/xraydata.ts:468,555-557,572`, `src/components/beamline-panel.tsx:48`. Keep the heuristic mode decision and all numbers unchanged. Add a gas-path regression test. The policy of always recommending fluorescence below 2 keV remains a rule of thumb, not a proven optimum.

No correction was indicated for these conventions:

- Mass fractions use atom counts times molar masses; compound attenuation is the mass-weighted sum times density. Thickness conversion is `d_cm = d_um * 1e-4`. The planner targets `Delta mu * d = 1` and caps `mu_total * d` at 3. The 13 mm die area is `pi * 0.65^2` cm². [NIST compound additivity](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/XrayMassCoef/intro.html).
- Independent Poisson draws have the prescribed means, support zero counts, and can give apparent transmission above one. Zero-count logarithms remain undefined. The instrument drawing is an idealized count model, not an ion-chamber electronics/noise model. [NIST Poisson definition](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda366j.htm).
- `C(t)` uses negative excitation-gap phases; reconstruction uses a positive Fourier exponential, `1/pi`, trapezoidal endpoints and `exp(-eta*t)`. Thus `eta` is Lorentzian HWHM, not FWHM. `dt = T/(N-1)`, Nyquist energy `pi/dt`, padded spacing `2*pi/(N*padding*dt)` and nominal finite-time scale `2*pi/T` match the implementation. The cosine taper adds broadening. The toy response lacks the physical dipole and absorption prefactors, as disclosed; it is not an absolute XAS cross section. [Fomichev 2025, time-domain formulation](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15784v2).
- The time conversion is approximately 0.6582119569 fs per inverse eV when hbar is set to one. This follows from the SI Planck constant and electronvolt conversion. [NIST constants](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/index.html).
- Charge balance `v_Mn = 4-x`, the conditional Mn(III)/Mn(IV) mixture, dipole `Delta l = +/-1`, and the distinction between the MnO6 sketch and oxygen-centred paper example are appropriate. The paper's orbital count is 10 Mn 3d + 4 Li 2s + 3 O 2p + 1 O 1s = 18 spatial orbitals, 22 electrons; 36 system qubits assumes a direct spin-orbital mapping, before reductions/ancillas. [Fomichev 2024, section IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.11015v1#S4).
- Derivative coefficients match the three-point nonuniform formula; endpoint normalization is explicitly a first/last-row-window operation. Fits report data-minus-model residuals, a nonnegative scale, constant baseline and a discrete shift search. The reduced chi-square caveat acknowledges its approximate degrees-of-freedom interpretation. Similarity scores do not infer valence.
- The cited research-panel arXiv identifiers resolve to the named topics/titles, including the 2026 references. They do not establish that those algorithms are implemented here. Density-source completeness remains item 18.
